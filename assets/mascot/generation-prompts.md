## Icon portrait prompt

Reference: the two-frame illustrated frog source. Transparent background disabled.

Edit the referenced approved Keroo frog illustration into ONE square high-resolution application icon. Use the LEFT OPEN-EYED illustrated frog as the exact identity reference, NOT the sleepy right one. Preserve its face, warm green paper texture, cream eye whites, deep green glossy eyes, slightly asymmetric friendly smile, little nostrils, peach blush, organic forest-green outlines and painted shadow treatment. This is a faithful close-up portrait of that same character, not a new design or a flat SVG/vector simplification. Square icon composition with the head, eye bumps, cheeks and only a little cream chest visible; NO coin, hands, arms, legs, cups or other props. Head and cheeks occupy about 85% of the square canvas, very readable in tiny taskbar sizes, centered with 7% breathing room at sides and above both eye bumps, no cut-off eye bumps or awkward cropping. Put it on a perfectly flat pale warm green #E7EFD5 square background, full bleed to all four edges, NO rounded corner mask baked in, no outer border or shadows. The exporter will add masks per platform. ONE face only, no grid, no multiple sizes, no text or watermark. Retain the exact appealing illustrative texture and dimensional color of the approved artwork. Output ideally 1024x1024 or higher.
